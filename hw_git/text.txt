Lionel Messi is an Argentine footballer.
He was born on 24 June 1987.
He made his debut for Barcelona in 2004.
Messi won multiple Ballon d'Or awards.
He helped Barcelona win many trophies.
He played for the Argentina national team.
Argentina won the 2022 FIFA World Cup.
Messi later joined Inter Miami.
He is known for his passing and dribbling.
Many fans consider him one of football's greatest players.
